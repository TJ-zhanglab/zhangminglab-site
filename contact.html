Join us

Join us to work on frontier AI models and innovate drug development for disease!

Undergraduates, master's and doctoral applicants, and postdoctoral researchers with an interest in brain science, genomics, deep learning or bioengineering are all welcomed to apply.

The lab suits people who want to develop AI models to innovate genomics research and translate the findings into medicine in neurodegenerative diseases. You do not need to arrive fluent in both computational or biological sides. The curiosity and ambition is the drive.

Open positions

What we are looking for

Graduate study

Master's and PhD applicants

Applications are welcomed from students interested in neurological disease, deep learning algorithms or bioengineering. Application should go through the Tongji University School of Medicine admission processes and contact us at the same time.

Postdoctoral

Postdoctoral researchers

For candidates with a background in computational genomics, statistics, AI/machine learning, or neurological diseaes/neuroscience. Include a CV and a short note on the direction you want to take.

Undergraduate

Undergraduate projects

Short projects for undergraduates who want exposure to genomics or model development before committing to graduate study.

Write to mingzhang@tongji.edu.cn with a CV, a transcript if you are a student, and a paragraph on what you would like to work on. Naming a specific paper or project helps.
