Principal investigator

Ming Zhang 张明

PhD, Principal Investigator. Tenured Associate Professor, Clinical Research Center for Brain and Spinal Cord, School of Medicine, Tongji University.

mingzhang@tongji.edu.cn

Research interests
  • Deep learning models for genomics, RNA biology and spatial omics
  • Multi-omics of neurodegenerative disease — genomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics
  • Single-cell and spatial mechanisms of ALS and other neurodegenerative diseases

Record

53 SCI-indexed papers, 30 as first or corresponding author, cited more than 3,100 times; h-index 32 (Google Scholar). Findings were published in Acta Neuropathologica, Brain, eBioMedicine, American Journal of Human Genetics, Movement Disorders, Neurology and JNNP.

Principal investigator on multiple grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Commission. Contributed to a graduate textbook on medical molecular genetics (Fudan University Press, 2022).

Service

Reviewer for Nature Communications, Acta Neuropathologica, Movement Disorders, Journal of Alzheimer's Disease and Neurobiology of Aging. Invited grant reviewer for the Alzheimer's Society UK and the MND Association.
