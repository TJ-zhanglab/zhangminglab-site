Research

Four questions we are working on

Which RNAs a cell makes, and what they do, is set by the genome and the proteome acting together. That control erodes with age, and its failure drives neurodegeneration.

01 / Genome

The genetic code of RNA regulation

How does DNA sequence specify RNA diversity and function?

02 / Proteome

The protein code of RNA regulation

How do RNA-binding proteins shape RNA diversity and function?

03 / Interaction

Where the two meet

How do RNA–protein interactions drive key biological processes, and how does their dysfunction drive disease? TDP-43 is our worked example.

04 / Models

Whether AI can read it

Can models learn the nucleic acid code well enough to predict RNA function and interpret its grammar — and then to design nucleic acid drugs?

Open source

AI models that predict and interpret the RNA code

Spliformer reads pre-mRNA sequence and shows which motifs it attended to. Spliformer-V2 predicts splice site usage across 18 human tissues, twelve of them CNS. SpatialE finds where in a tissue a gene set concentrates. All three are open, and two run in the browser.
